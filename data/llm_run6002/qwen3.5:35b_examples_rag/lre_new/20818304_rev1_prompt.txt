### Process description:
The credit company receives the credit information from the customer, If the requested amount is greater than 1M$ an approval must be requested. If the requested amount is lower or equal to 1M$ the company assess the risk of the credit. After the assessment, if the risk is high, an approval must be requested; but if the risk is low the credit is accepted. After the approval request, the credit could be accepted or rejected; in both cases, an email is sent to the customer.
