### Process description:
The account creation process at this bank proceeds as follows. The first steps in the process are performed by the bank customer. After visiting the bank website, she fills in her personal data and generates a PDF document with all the information relevant to this account request. After printing this PDF, the customer needs to visit a post office to send these documents. The post officer will check the identity of the customer and mail the account to the bank. At the bank, the person in charge of making a decision on account requests is the bank officer. Once he receives a new request, he'll check the customer data. Depending on the available information and his own criteria, he may either accept or reject the request. In the former case, he will mail the account documents to the customer. In the latter, he will mail a letter of refusal.

[DESCRIPTION:]
A client who wants to create a Paypal account first needs to determine the desired account type.
He should either select a personal account or a premium account.
Afterwards, the client should enter login details, such as e-mail address and desired password, personal details, such as name and date of birth, as well as his telephone number and address.
Finally, the client needs to declare if he has been affiliated with any politically exposed person.
When the client clicks the create account button, the details will be sent to the Paypal system.
When a request is received, the system will validate the input fields.
If there are any invalid fields, the client will be notified and needs to correct them.
Otherwise a welcome email will be generated and sent to indicate that an account has been created.
